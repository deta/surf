## 📊 Main Presentation File

### Hypocalcemia_Pediatric_Patients.pptx

**File Details**:
- **Size**: 259 KB
- **Format**: Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx)
- **Slides**: 12
- **Duration**: 25-30 minutes
- **Compatibility**: PowerPoint 2010+, Google Slides, Keynote

**Slide Contents**:
1. Title Slide - Professional introduction
2. Agenda - Presentation roadmap
3. Introduction to Hypocalcemia - Definition and epidemiology
4. Pathophysiology - Calcium homeostasis mechanisms
5. Causes in Pediatric Patients - 5 categories with examples
6. Clinical Presentation - Symptoms by body system
7. Diagnosis - Laboratory tests and imaging
8. Management and Treatment - Acute and chronic protocols
9. Complications & Prevention - Outcomes and strategies
10. Case Study - Real-world clinical scenario
11. Key Takeaways - 5 essential points
12. Conclusion & Q&A - References and discussion

**Design Features**:
- Modern medical theme (blue and teal)
- Professional typography (Arial)
- Color-coded categories
- Visual flowcharts and diagrams
- Clean, readable layout
